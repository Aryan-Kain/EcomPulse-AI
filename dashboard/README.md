\# ⚡ What is EcomPulse AI?



> \*\*EcomPulse AI is an AI-powered e-commerce intelligence platform designed to transform complex business data into clear, visual, and actionable insights.\*\*



Modern e-commerce platforms generate huge amounts of data from \*\*customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and deliveries\*\*.



But raw CSV files don't immediately answer important questions.



\*\*EcomPulse AI helps answer them.\*\*



<div align="center">



\### 📊 ANALYZE \&nbsp; ➜ \&nbsp; 🧠 UNDERSTAND \&nbsp; ➜ \&nbsp; 💡 DISCOVER \&nbsp; ➜ \&nbsp; 🚀 DECIDE



</div>

\## 🎯 Why Was It Built?



The purpose of EcomPulse AI is to demonstrate how \*\*data analytics, business intelligence, AI-oriented insights, and modern web development\*\* can work together to make e-commerce data easier to understand.



Instead of searching through multiple datasets, the platform brings important business intelligence into one centralized dashboard.



\### The platform helps answer:



<table>

<td width="50%">



\### 💰 How is the business performing?
